# Woodworth (1899), Tables I to IV, right hand

Source: Woodworth, R. S. (1899). The accuracy of voluntary movement. Psychological Review Monograph Supplements, 3 (Whole No. 13). Scanned copy held by the author (file 2011-13573-001.pdf); the PDF itself is not redistributed.

What the values are: average error in mm when drawing a line equal to the previous one, right hand, at metronome rates of 20 to 200 movements per minute, eyes open and eyes shut. One table per participant (Tables I, II, III, IV). Two eyes-shut cells of Table III are blank in the original and are left blank here.

Where: Tables I and II on p. 34, Tables III and IV on p. 35 of the monograph (pages 40 and 41 of the scanned file). The first column, headed MM., gives the rate in movements per minute set by a metronome, as described in the text. Only the right-hand eyes-open and eyes-shut columns are transcribed.

How they were obtained: read from the scan on 2 November 2026, and read a second time, independently and value by value, from the 300 dpi page images on 4 November 2026. The two readings agree in all 80 cells.
