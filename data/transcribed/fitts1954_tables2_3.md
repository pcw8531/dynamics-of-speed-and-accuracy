# Fitts (1954), Tables 2 and 3

Source: Fitts, P. M. (1954). Journal of Experimental Psychology, 47(6), 381–391. PDF held by the author, not redistributed.

Table 2, p. 386: "Task conditions and performance data for 16 variations of a disc-transfer task (N = the same 16 Ss at each condition)". W is the difference in inches between the diameter of the post and the diameter of the centre hole of the disc; A is the centre-to-centre distance between the posts; t is the average time in seconds for a movement between two posts. No errors were permitted. Times are averaged over both days, with a 6% decrease on the second set of trials (p. 386).

Table 3, p. 388: "Task conditions and performance data for 20 variations of a pin-transfer task (N = the same 20 Ss at each condition)". W is the difference in inches between the diameter of the pins and the diameter of the hole; t is for the combined second and third replications. Over-all means for the three days were .613, .585 and .578 s per movement (p. 387).
