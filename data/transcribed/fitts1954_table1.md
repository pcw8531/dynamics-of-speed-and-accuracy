# Fitts (1954), Table 1

Source: Fitts, P. M. (1954). The information capacity of the human motor system in controlling the amplitude of movement. Journal of Experimental Psychology, 47(6), 381–391. Table 1, p. 385, "Task conditions and performance data for 16 variations of a reciprocal tapping task (N = the same 16 Ss at each condition)". PDF held by the author, not redistributed.

Columns: W_in, target width in inches; A_in, distance between target centres in inches; Id_bits, index of difficulty log2(2A/W); t, average time in seconds for one movement; errors, percent; Ip, the index of performance in bits per second as printed; rank, as printed. Suffix 1oz and 1lb mark the two styluses.
